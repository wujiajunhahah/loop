What exactly is Wozai?

Wozai is a relationship-work service for living creators. You can preserve authentic content in your own voice, video, photos, or words, confirm it personally, authorize it by relationship, and let it be seen and used with evidence and care in the future.

How is it different from digital immortality or a digital human?

We do not create digital resurrection, personality replacement, or open-ended chats with the deceased. The system only cites, organizes, and connects content you confirmed. When evidence is insufficient, it declines instead of pretending that you are replying in real time.

Will AI say things I never said?

No. Original material is always preserved, and AI organization is clearly distinguished from your own words. Organized content must be confirmed by you before delivery, and every future memory traces back to the original text, voice, image, or video.

Can I leave a piece of content for only one person?

Yes. Each piece can have its own recipient, visibility, scope, and intensity. Family status does not grant automatic access; unauthorized people will neither see it nor be told that it exists.

Must the recipient view it?

No. Recipients can decline, pause, reduce intensity, turn off sound, or permanently stop reminders. Whether and when to view is entirely their choice.

Can I use Wozai when I have limited energy?

Yes. Wozai is designed for short, interruptible recording. You only need to say a little at a time; the system saves, transcribes, and organizes it. Stop whenever you wish and continue later.

Will content be stored forever?

We will not make an unrealistic promise of permanent storage. The formal service will clearly state its custody period, full export, migration, and shutdown plans before you authorize it.

Is Wozai a psychotherapy service?

No. We do not diagnose emotions, assess stages of grief, or promise healing. Wozai's boundaries are authentic records, permission-based delivery, and restrained relationship support, with human and professional resources where needed.
