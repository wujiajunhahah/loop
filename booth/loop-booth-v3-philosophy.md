# Tender Provenance

Tender Provenance treats memory as something both intimate and observable. Space is deep, quiet, and deliberate; a small human signal sits inside a much larger field, protected rather than displayed. Circular boundaries and orbital paths express relationship, permission, and return without explaining them literally. Every curve is meticulously placed so the composition feels discovered through patient observation rather than assembled from components.

Color behaves as evidence. Warm coral marks the living source, cool mint marks consent and safe passage, and paper white marks what can be inspected and trusted. These colors remain scarce against midnight blue, gaining meaning through restraint. Their transitions should feel painstakingly calibrated, like light measured in a dark laboratory by someone at the top of their craft.

Scale carries the emotional argument. One monumental relational system dominates the canvas while tiny reference marks, coordinates, and labels imply provenance. Repetition forms a quiet rhythm: many possible memories, one approved path, one chosen recipient. The work rewards distance with a clear silhouette and proximity with precise details, each executed with master-level attention.

Typography is evidence, not narration. A single bold proposition establishes the work; everything else appears as sparse identifiers, short human statements, and clinical annotations. Serif type carries memory and tenderness, while condensed sans-serif type carries systems and accountability. Alignment, spacing, and line breaks must look labored over with care, with no accidental edge or decorative filler.

The composition balances emotional asymmetry with scientific order. The source is not centered, because memory moves between people; the receiving point is given visible agency, not treated as an endpoint. Negative space is active and protective. The finished artifact should feel like a museum-grade map of an invisible relationship: sophisticated, quiet, and meticulously crafted through countless refinements.
