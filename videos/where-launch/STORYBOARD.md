## Video direction

- Palette: `frame.md` is normative. Cream is the default product-evidence ground; deep green carries the opening and final gravity moments; rain blue and coral are rationed as focal accents. Every frame uses the editorial mono topbar and no more than two dominant surfaces.
- Type: display roles carry the Chinese statements at frame scale; body roles explain only what the real UI proves; mono roles carry `ORIGINAL SOURCE`, `AI GENERATED`, `CONTEXT ID`, and `PULL_ONLY` as inspection chrome.
- Motion grammar: smooth long-tail settles, camera moves only to establish or target a real UI region, and every later claim appears in sequence across the shot. Silent-video cues replace VO cues: each line earns its own reading window. No bounce, lazy breathing, infinite motion, or back-half camera drift.
- Rhythm: Frames 1 and 4 develop continuously; Frames 2, 3, and 5 settle into evidence holds; Frame 6 is the deliberate breather and longest final read. All load-bearing content stays above the bottom 17% keep-out band.
- Negative list: no gradients, glow, shadows, invented app screens, atmospheric stock footage, digital-resurrection language, slideshow front-loading, or screensaver-style independent floating elements.
